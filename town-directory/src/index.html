HOW IT WORKS

From empty world to living campaign in four steps.

  1. 1

    Build your towns & world

    Add settlements, set demographics & rules per town, upload your world map, and drop pins on the locations that matter to your party.

  2. 2

    Run a month between sessions

    Simulate one town or every settlement in a single pass. Births, arrivals, departures, relationships, drama, and the occasional disaster—all configurable.

  3. 3

    Anchor everything to the calendar

    Every event lands on a real campaign date. Hook plots to specific moons, festivals, or seasons your players already recognize.

  4. 4

    Keep the lore current

    The AI scribe writes wiki entries from the events that just happened, so your codex grows with the campaign instead of after it.

FEATURES

A campaign manager, a world simulator, and a lore engine.

🌍 Town & World Simulation

Configurable rates for birth, death, conflict, and relationship formation. Run 0–24 months per pass; preview changes before applying.

🗺️ Geographic World Maps

Upload your own map, place pins for towns and landmarks, and estimate travel distance and duration between any two points.

📅 In-Game Calendar

Custom calendar config. Advance days, weeks, or months. Sims and plot hooks tie back to campaign dates automatically.

📜 AI Scribe & Wiki

Auto-generate town histories, NPC bios, and recap entries. Searchable, editable wiki you can share with your table.

🧙 Characters & Level-Up

Full character sheets plus a guided level-up wizard (3.5e most complete). Roster import from statblocks.

⚔️ Encounters

Build encounters from your roster and the SRD; track combat state in a dedicated view.

🎮 VTT Export

Export rosters and characters in formats your virtual tabletop can read.

👥 Player Portal

Optional read-only view your players can bookmark—just the lore and campaign info you choose to share.

💬 Discord Integration

Deploy notices, scribe pings, and bug reports route to your Discord. Optional and configurable.

EDITIONS

Built around 3.5e. Friendly to the rest.

Eon Weaver's roster, sim, calendar, and scribe are edition-agnostic. The character sheet and level-up wizard go deepest on 3.5e today; other editions are usable now and growing.
